🎯How do you match my child with the right tutor?

We take matching seriously! Our team considers your child's year level, subjects, learning style, and personality to find their perfect tutor. It's like assembling a dream team -- we handpick someone who clicks with how your child learns best.

💰How much does Tutero cost?

Lessons start from $50/hour depending on subject and year level. Every lesson is 1-on-1 and fully personalised. We also offer sibling discounts and flexible packages.

🧑‍🏫What qualifications do your tutors have?

All tutors hold a Working With Children Check and go through rigorous screening. They scored in the top percentiles in the subjects they teach -- and we train them to make learning fun.

📅Can we reschedule or cancel lessons?

Life happens! Reschedule or cancel with 24 hours' notice at no charge. Learning shouldn't feel like a rigid contract.

🚀How quickly will I see results?

Most parents see a confidence boost within 2-3 lessons. Grade improvements follow within the first month. We build understanding from the ground up.

🌏Are lessons online or in-person?

All lessons happen online via our interactive classroom -- whiteboard, screen sharing, real-time problem solving. Your child learns from home. Best tutors across Australia, not just your postcode.
