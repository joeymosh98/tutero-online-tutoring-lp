The most selective hiring in the industry.

Teaching a child who learns differently — well, with patience, with the right strategies, on a Tuesday afternoon when they've already had a hard day at school — is one of the hardest jobs in education. Most adults can't do it.

So we made the hiring process the hardest in the industry. Every year, more than 30,000 educators apply to teach with Tutero. We accept 1.3%. Every one of them passes a teaching exam, a Working with Children Check, and an interview with a working teacher before they're matched with a child. Before they take on a student with a learning difference, they're trained — properly, not in a one-hour video — on how to actually teach that child.

It's why families stay. It's why we don't need contracts.

What the first month looks like

1

The first call.

A real person from our team — not a chatbot — calls you within one business day. We ask about your child. Their diagnosis, their history, what's worked, what's made it worse. About fifteen minutes.

2

The match.

We hand-match your child to one teacher chosen for the way they learn. You meet them before any lesson is booked. If it doesn't feel right, we re-match.

3

The first lessons.

Online, one-on-one. The first sessions are mostly about your teacher learning your child. Real teaching starts when trust does.

4

The reports.

A written lesson report after every session. Your dedicated Account Manager — the same human every time — checks in regularly. If anything isn't working, we fix it that week.

You get a personal account manager — with you for every step.

Questions families ask us

This is the question we hear most, and the one we plan for from the first lesson. Your teacher's first job isn't curriculum — it's rapport. The first few sessions are deliberately low-pressure: getting to know your child, finding what they care about, earning the right to teach them. If after a few weeks the chemistry still isn't there, we re-match. There's no contract, no penalty, and no awkward conversation — we'd rather get it right than keep you in something that isn't working.
For most kids who learn differently, one-on-one online works better than in-person tutoring — no travel, no stranger in the house, no unfamiliar room, and the lesson can pause the second your child needs it to. For some children, it isn't the right fit, and we'll tell you honestly which group we think your child is in on the first call. We'd rather lose a customer than start something that isn't going to work.
Our teachers are trained across autism, ADHD, dyslexia, dyscalculia, dysgraphia, auditory processing differences, anxiety, and the combinations most kids actually have. We don't match every teacher to every child — we hand-match based on what your child needs. Tell us about your child on the first call and we'll put you with someone who has taught a child like yours before, or we'll tell you we're not the right fit.
We hear this on roughly half our calls. Most of the time, what didn't work was a uni student doing your child's homework with them — well-meaning, untrained, and unaccountable. Tutero teachers are qualified, trained for learning differences, and watched closely by a dedicated Account Manager who reads every lesson report. If it's not working, we see it before you have to tell us, and we fix it.
Tutero is not currently an NDIS provider, so sessions can't be claimed under an NDIS plan. We're a private teaching practice paid for directly by families. We'd rather you knew that upfront than after a phone call.
We'll give you exact numbers on the first call, because the right plan depends on your child. As a starting point: a year of Tutero costs less than a single term at most Australian private schools, with no contract and the option to pause any week. We're not the cheapest option in the market and we don't try to be — the families who stay with us are the ones who've tried cheaper and decided it wasn't worth it.
Maths is our strongest. We also teach English, science, and broader literacy and numeracy support across primary and high school. If your child needs help in a subject we don't teach well, we'll tell you on the first call.
Most families have their first lesson within one to two weeks of the initial call. We don't rush the matching process — finding the right teacher for a child who learns differently takes a few days, and we'd rather get it right than get it fast.
