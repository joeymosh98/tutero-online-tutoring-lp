Frequently asked questions

Do your tutors have experience with ADHD, autism and dyslexia?
Yes. Every Tutero tutor declares the specific learning needs they're trained and experienced in, and we match your child only with one verified for theirs — whether that's ADHD, autism, dyslexia, dyscalculia or anxiety.
How much does it cost?
Lessons start from $65/hour for 1-on-1 online tutoring. There are no upfront costs and you pay after each lesson — so there's nothing to lose trying it.
How do you match my child with the right tutor?
We learn about your child's specific needs, diagnosis, year level and goals, then hand-pick a tutor from our vetted pool with the right experience and personality. You meet them before any lesson is booked.
How quickly will we see a difference?
Most parents notice a confidence shift within the first few lessons — calmer about homework and more willing to try. Grade improvements typically follow over the first term.
Are lessons online or in person?
All lessons are online through our interactive classroom. For many children with special needs, learning from a calm, familiar space at home improves focus and reduces anxiety compared to a tutoring centre.
What if the tutor isn't the right fit?
Just let your account manager know and we'll re-match your child straight away — no questions asked. Finding the right fit matters most for children with special needs, and we take that seriously.
