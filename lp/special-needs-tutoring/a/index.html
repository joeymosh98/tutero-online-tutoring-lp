Got questions? We've got answers

Curiosity level:
🤔
🧩Do your tutors have experience with ADHD, autism, and dyslexia?

Yes — we specifically match students with tutors who have experience supporting their condition. Whether it's ADHD, ASD, dyslexia, dyscalculia, or anxiety, we only pair your child with an educator who understands how they learn and how to keep them engaged.

💰How much does it cost?

Lessons start from $65/hour for 1-on-1 online tutoring. There are no upfront costs and you pay after each lesson — so there's nothing to lose trying it.

🎯How do you match my child with the right tutor?

When you enquire, our team learns about your child's specific needs, diagnosis, year level, and goals. We then handpick a tutor from our vetted pool who has the right experience and personality for your child. We don't guess — we match carefully.

⏳️How quickly will I see results?

Most parents report a confidence boost within 2–3 lessons. For students with ADHD or anxiety, just having a patient 1-on-1 environment makes a visible difference early. Grade improvements typically follow within the first 4–6 weeks.

📍Are lessons online or in-person?

All lessons are online via our interactive classroom — whiteboard, screen sharing, and real-time tools. For students with special needs, learning from home in a familiar, calm environment often improves focus and reduces anxiety compared to in-person settings.

🔄What if the tutor isn't the right fit?

We'll find you a new one — no questions asked. Finding the right match is especially important for children with special needs, and we take that seriously. Just let your account manager know and we'll re-match immediately.
